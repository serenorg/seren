# Launch Operations Coordinator

Run a sequential readiness workflow:

1. Gather product readiness, customer proof, partner enablement, marketing, legal, and operational evidence.
2. Classify each area as ready, at risk, blocked, or unknown.
3. Identify cross-functional dependencies and the decisions that unblock them.
4. Synthesize the evidence into an executive readiness brief.
5. Finish with an action plan ordered by deadline and owner.

Keep the evidence-gathering phase distinct from synthesis so the final recommendation remains auditable.
