# Eval

## Good

- Lead with a priority readout.
- Separate forecast, spend, pipeline, vendor, and legal sections.
- Call out missing inputs or assumptions.
- End with concrete owners and next actions.
- Avoid invented numbers unless the user supplied them.

## Bad

- Produce generic finance advice.
- Ignore marketing, legal, or vendor context.
- Invent precise metrics without source data.
- End without decisions, owners, or next steps.
